## House style (every photo)
Square 1:1, premium e-commerce lifestyle photo, warm soft morning light, rustic dark wooden table, shallow depth of
field, photorealistic, appetising. The product itself in a bowl/pot/basket beside its pack. Background must suit the
product (paddy field for rice; millet field for millets; palm grove for palm products; sesame plants or a wooden
chekku oil press for gingelly oil; soft kitchen for powders). No other text anywhere.

## Packs
- Naadu Nalam products: matte kraft stand-up pouch with a clean white front label that shows the Naadu Nalam logo
  (the round logo with leaves, sun, soil and water — reproduce faithfully), the product name in dark green below
  it, and the pack size small below that (exactly as given, e.g. "500 g" or "1 kg").
- Thaayanbu products: kraft-paper label (or kraft tag) with a small green leaf mark, "Thaayanbu" in large dark green
  serif letters and the product name below.

## Workflow per photo
1. Click the "Enter a prompt for Gemini" textbox (use find to get its ref) BEFORE typing — stray keys trigger Gemini
   shortcuts. Type the prompt, press Return. Wait 40–60 s, screenshot.
2. CHECK: spelling of every word on the label, pack size, logo quality, correct product, right background. If wrong,
   ask Gemini to fix and wait again.
3. Download only the final correct version, exactly once: scroll so the image's top-right icons are visible,
   screenshot, then left_click the download icon (the down-arrow, rightmost of the three) with a REAL click.
   Never download with JavaScript. Wait 6 s after each click. Then continue.
4. Keep a list in order: subject + exact label text. Your final report = that ordered list + anything that failed.
