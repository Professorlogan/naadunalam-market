<h1> Collaborators 
<p> Naadu Nalam is a marketplace, not a warehouse. Everything on the site comes from a seller who grows, presses, mills or gathers it themselves. This page is where those collaborators — and the community organisations working alongside them — will be introduced.

<h2> How collaboration works here 
<p> Three things are true of every seller on the marketplace today. 
<h3> Every seller has their own store 
<p> Sellers list and manage their own products, stock and orders, and each one has a store page of their own on this site. 
<h3> Listed in English and Tamil 
<p> Product names and descriptions carry both, so the produce is named the way the people who grow it name it. 
<h3> One basket, many sellers 
<p> You can order from several sellers in a single basket. Orders on the marketplace are Cash on Delivery at present.
