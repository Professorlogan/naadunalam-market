<h2> 1. Order processing 
<p> Processing begins once your order has been placed and confirmed on the website. You will see your order details and the total payable, including delivery, before you confirm. 
<p> Orders are packed within 24–48 hours on business days (Monday–Saturday) . Orders placed on Sunday are packed on the next business day. 
<p> Fresh fruits, vegetables and frozen items are bought against the day’s orders. These items are stocked in limited daily quantities and may sell out. Delivery slots for perishable orders may be confirmed by SMS or email. 
<h2> 2. Shipping and delivery 
<p> Naadu Nalam delivers across Chennai and select locations in Tamil Nadu, and ships non-perishable items more widely. 
<li> Chennai and surrounding pin codes — 1–3 business days 
<li> Other Tamil Nadu metros — 3–5 business days 
<li> Rest of India — 5–8 business days, non-perishable items only 
<p> Fresh fruits, vegetables and frozen items are delivered only within Chennai and selected Tamil Nadu locations. If your pin code is outside our fresh-delivery area, perishable items will not be available at checkout. 
<p> Fresh fruits, vegetables and frozen items are packed in insulated packaging and handled on priority. 
<p> Receiving your order. Please make sure someone is available to receive the order at the delivery address, particularly for fresh and frozen items. If a delivery cannot be completed because no one is available, we will attempt to contact you to reschedule. Perishable items that cannot be delivered after a failed attempt may not be re-dispatched. 
<p> Delivery timelines may vary depending on the delivery location and other circumstances. The estimated delivery information shown during checkout will apply where available. 
<h2> 3. Shipping charges 
<p> We charge a flat delivery fee of ₹50 per order , on every order and to every serviceable location. There are no additional handling or packaging charges. 
<p> The delivery fee is shown separately in your cart and again on the checkout page before you confirm the order. 
<h2> 4. Delivery delays 
<p> The timelines above are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery may be affected by circumstances outside our reasonable control, including: 
<li> Weather conditions 
<li> Local restrictions 
<li> Courier or transport disruption 
<li> Operational issues 
<li> An incorrect or incomplete delivery address 
<p> If your order is delayed beyond the stated window, contact us using the details below and we will trace it for you. 
<h2> 5. Incorrect or incomplete address 
<p> Please check your delivery address, pin code and phone number carefully before confirming an order. An incorrect or incomplete address may delay delivery or prevent it from being completed. 
<p> If you notice an error after placing an order, contact us as soon as possible and we will try to update the details before the order is dispatched. 
<p> Where a delivery cannot be completed because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will attempt to contact you to arrange redelivery. As noted above, perishable items that cannot be delivered after a failed attempt may not be re-dispatched. 
<h2> 6. Damaged, spoiled or incorrect orders 
<p> If an order arrives damaged, spoiled or incorrect, please report it within 24 hours of delivery . 
<p> Please contact us as soon as possible with your order details and relevant photographs where applicable. This helps us resolve the issue quickly and, where needed, raise it with the seller or delivery partner.
