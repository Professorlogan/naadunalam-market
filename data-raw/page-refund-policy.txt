<h2> 1. Eligibility 
<p> The following may be eligible for a refund or replacement: 
<li> Damaged, spoiled or incorrect items — reported within 24 hours of delivery . 
<li> Non-perishable products — unopened and in their original packaging, reported within 7 days . 
<p> This policy applies to marketplace orders in the same way as to items we supply directly, including items supplied by independent sellers on our marketplace. 
<h2> 2. Damaged or spoiled products 
<p> If an item arrives damaged or spoiled, report it within 24 hours of delivery. Please send us: 
<li> Your order number 
<li> The product name and details 
<li> Photographs or a short video of the issue, where applicable 
<li> A brief description of what is wrong 
<p> Photographs help us resolve the issue quickly and, where needed, raise it with the seller or delivery partner. 
<h2> 3. Incorrect product 
<p> If you receive a product different from what you ordered, report it within 24 hours of delivery with your order number, the item you received and a photograph where possible. 
<p> Where stock allows, we may offer a replacement of the correct item instead of a refund. If a replacement is not available, an approved refund will be processed as set out below. 
<h2> 4. Quality concerns 
<p> If you have a genuine concern about the quality or condition of an item you received, tell us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number, photographs where applicable and a description of the problem. We will review each case individually. 
<p> Fresh produce is a natural product, so size, colour and appearance will vary from the photographs shown on the website. Natural variation of this kind is not in itself a quality defect. 
<h2> 5. Non-returnable items 
<p> The following are not eligible for return: 
<li> Fresh fruits, vegetables and frozen items, once delivered in acceptable condition. 
<li> Products that have been consumed, or stored improperly after delivery. 
<p> This does not affect your rights where an item was damaged, spoiled or incorrect on delivery and reported within 24 hours. 
<h2> 6. Refunds 
<p> Requests are reviewed individually against the eligibility rules above. Once a refund is approved: 
<li> Orders paid online — the refund is returned to the original payment method within 5–7 business days . 
<li> Cash on Delivery orders — there is no original payment method to refund to, so we will ask you for a UPI ID or bank account and process the refund there within the same 5–7 business days . 
<p> The time taken for the amount to appear in your account after we process it may depend on your bank or payment provider. 
<h2> 7. Replacements 
<p> Where stock allows, we may offer a replacement instead of a refund for damaged or incorrect items. We will confirm this with you before arranging it. 
<h2> 8. Order cancellation 
<p> If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your order number. 
<p> Orders are packed within 24–48 hours on business days, and fresh items are bought against the day’s orders, so cancellation may not be possible once an order has been packed or dispatched. 
<p> We may also refuse or cancel an order ourselves for fraud prevention, stock availability or delivery restrictions, as set out in our  {a:https://naadunalam.fastfilings.in/terms/} Terms of Service . Where we do, anything you have already paid for that order is refunded.
