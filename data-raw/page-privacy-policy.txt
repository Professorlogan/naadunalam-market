<h2> 1. Information we collect 
<li> Order information — your name, delivery address, pin code, email address and phone number, collected at checkout so we can process, pack, deliver and invoice your order. 
<li> Account information — if you create an account, your username, email address and saved delivery addresses. 
<li> Enquiry information — the name, email address and message you submit through our contact form. 
<li> Technical information — your IP address, browser type and pages viewed, recorded in standard server logs and used to keep the site secure and working correctly. 
<h2> 2. How we use your information 
<li> To process, pack and deliver your order, and to contact you about it. 
<li> To handle refunds, replacements and delivery issues under our  {a:https://naadunalam.fastfilings.in/refund-policy/} Refund Policy . 
<li> To respond to enquiries you send us. 
<li> To manage your account, where you have created one. 
<li> To keep records required for tax and accounting purposes. 
<li> To detect and prevent fraud and misuse of the website. 
<p> We send marketing email only if you have asked us to. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not affect order-related messages. 
<h2> 3. Payment information 
<p> We do not collect or store your card, UPI or net-banking details. 
<p> Where an online payment method is offered, payment is completed on the payment provider’s own secure systems and we receive only a confirmation of the result. 
<p> Where you choose Cash on Delivery, payment is collected at the time your order is handed to you. 
<h2> 4. Cookies 
<p> We use cookies that are necessary for the site to function, including keeping items in your cart and keeping you signed in. 
<p> Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Please note that blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the website, such as the cart and checkout, from working correctly. 
<h2> 5. Who we share it with 
<p> We share your information only where it is needed to run the service: 
<li> Delivery partners — your name, address and phone number, so your order can be delivered. 
<li> Sellers on our marketplace — where an item is supplied by an independent seller, the details needed to prepare and dispatch that item. 
<li> Payment providers — where you pay online, to process and verify the transaction. 
<li> Our hosting and IT providers — who store and process data on our behalf under contract. 
<li> Authorities — where we are required to disclose information by law. 
<p> We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising. 
<h2> 6. Keeping your information secure 
<p> This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to order and customer data is limited to people who need it to fulfil orders and support customers. 
<p> No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. 
<h2> 7. How long we keep it 
<li> Order and invoice records are retained for as long as required under applicable tax and accounting rules. 
<li> Account details are kept until you ask us to delete your account. 
<li> Contact-form enquiries are kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry. 
<h2> 8. Your rights 
<p> Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to: 
<li> Confirm what personal data we hold about you and obtain a copy of it. 
<li> Correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. 
<li> Erase your personal data, where we are not required to retain it. 
<li> Withdraw a consent you previously gave, at any time. 
<li> Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. 
<p> To make a request, write to us using the contact details below. We will respond within a reasonable period. 
<h2> 9. Children 
<p> This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Orders should be placed by an adult.
